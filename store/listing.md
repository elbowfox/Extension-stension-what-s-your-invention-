## Full Description (up to 16,000 chars)

**Stop losing your browser sessions. Start saving them.**

TabFlow is the simplest, most powerful tab session manager for Chrome. With a single click, save all your open tabs as a named session — and restore them instantly whenever you need them.

### ✅ Features

**Save & Restore Sessions**
- Save all your open tabs as a named session in one click
- Restore any saved session — opens all tabs in one go
- Rename or delete sessions any time
- Quick keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+S to save, Ctrl+Shift+T to open TabFlow

**Tab Search**
- Instantly search through all your open tabs
- Click any result to jump directly to that tab

**Session Dashboard**
- See how many tabs you have open, sessions saved, and total tabs stored
- Visual favicon preview for each saved session

**Shopping Reminder**
- On checkout/cart pages, TabFlow gently reminds you to save your session before you buy — so you never lose your research tabs

**Clean, Minimal UI**
- Designed to be fast and non-intrusive
- Works beautifully in both Chrome and Edge
