Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

TabFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information TabFlow collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. What We Collect

TabFlow stores data locally on your device only using the browser's built-in chrome.storage.local API. This includes:

  • Saved tab sessions (tab titles and URLs you choose to save)
  • Extension preferences (e.g. whether the shopping reminder is enabled)
  • Pro license status (if you have upgraded)

TabFlow does not transmit any of this data to external servers.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information
  • We do not track your browsing history
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties
  • We do not use analytics or tracking pixels

3. Permissions Explained

  • tabs — needed to read open tab titles and URLs so you can save and restore sessions. Tab data stays on your device.
  • storage — needed to save your sessions and preferences locally in your browser.
  • alarms — used for background tasks (e.g. session reminders). No data leaves your device.

4. External Links

TabFlow may include links to the TabFlow website (tabflow.pro) and the Chrome Web Store. These sites have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

5. Pro Subscription

If you purchase a TabFlow Pro subscription, payment processing is handled by a third-party payment provider. We only receive confirmation of successful payment; we do not store payment card information.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the extension update notes and on this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@tabflow.pro.
